# Sources

This package is an illustrative example. No religious authority has reviewed or endorsed it.
This file lists the texts it relies on, which of them are bundled, and what may be quoted
from each.

## Bundled with the package

### The Baltimore Catechism No. 2

- **What it is.** A Catechism of Christian Doctrine, No. 2, prepared and enjoined by order of
  the Third Plenary Council of Baltimore and approved on 6 April 1885. 421 questions in 37
  lessons.
- **Where it is.** `skills/catechism-answers/references/baltimore-catechism-no-2.md`
- **Source.** A public-domain transcription, taken on 2026-11-21 (UTC), that follows a
  printing of 1977.
- **Status.** Public domain. It was published in the United States in 1885, and its
  copyright there has long expired.
- **What was left out.** The prayers and hymns printed with that edition, and three answers
  (254, 257 and 258) whose wording in that printing is later than 1885. So the copy has 421
  questions and 418 answers. The top of the file gives the details.
- **Quoting.** It may be quoted freely. Give the number of the question.

### The Apostles' Creed

- **Where it is.** `references/apostles-creed.md`
- **Source and status.** The answer to question 12 of the same catechism. Public domain on
  the same basis.

## Cited and linked, not bundled

### The Catechism of the Catholic Church

- **What it is.** The Church's present statement of its teaching.
- **Where it is.** https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM
- **Status.** Under copyright (Libreria Editrice Vaticana), so it is not reproduced here.
- **Quoting.** Point to it. Quote it only from the page itself, with the paragraph number,
  and never from memory as if the wording were exact.

### The Bible

- No translation is bundled. Most modern translations are under copyright.
- The Holy See publishes the New American Bible at
  https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0839/_INDEX.HTM
- **Quoting.** Quote Scripture only from a text you can open in the conversation, and name
  the translation.

## How the package's own statements were checked

Each statement under "The basics" in `VALUES.md` was compared with the bundled catechism:

| Statement | Questions |
|---|---|
| One God in three divine Persons | 21, 23, 27 |
| Jesus Christ, true God and true man | 62 |
| He suffered and died for our sins and rose on the third day | 83, 89 |
| Christ founded the Church | 120 |
| The Pope, the bishops, and the four marks of the Church | 117 to 119, 128 |
| Seven sacraments | 137 |
| The Eucharist | 238 |
| The Apostles' Creed holds the chief truths the Church teaches | 11, 12 |
| The two commandments and the Ten Commandments | 311, 312, 313 |

Two statements rest on the current Catechism and not on the bundled text: that "Extreme
Unction" is an older name for the Anointing of the Sick (paragraph 1512), and that the
current Catechism treats the necessity of the Church at more length (paragraphs 846 to 848).
Both were read on the Holy See's website on 2026-11-21 (UTC).
