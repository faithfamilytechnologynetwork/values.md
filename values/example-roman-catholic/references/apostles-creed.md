# The Apostles' Creed

I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, His
only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered
under Pontius Pilate, was crucified; died, and was buried. He descended into hell: the third
day He arose again from the dead: He ascended into heaven, and sitteth at the right hand of
God, the Father Almighty; from thence He shall come to judge the living and the dead. I
believe in the Holy Ghost, the Holy Catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness
of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.

## Source and status

**Source.** The answer to question 12 of the Baltimore Catechism No. 2 (A Catechism of
Christian Doctrine, No. 2, approved in 1885), as transcribed in Project Gutenberg ebook
no. 14552 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14552), copied on 2026-11-20. Only the line
breaks differ. The whole catechism is bundled with this package's `catechism-answers` skill.

**Status.** Public domain. This English wording was published in the United States in 1885,
and its copyright there has long expired.

**Wording today.** This is an older English translation. It says "Holy Ghost" where
translations in use today say "Holy Spirit". No translation of the present day is reproduced
here, because its copyright status has not been shown. The current Catechism of the Catholic
Church gives the creed, under the heading "The Credo", at
https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM.
